# Article body

Ponscade is a free-to-play arcade in the browser, built for Robinhood Chain and funded by the Pons family launchpad.

You do not connect a wallet. You type a username and a 0x address. That is your player card. One wallet gets one name, forever. Come back with the same pair. A second card on the same address is refused.

Each wallet gets 10 turns per UTC day. There are three cabinets on the floor:

- VOLT — eat orbs. Five clears a stage.
- BREAKER — clear the wall. The wall gets denser.
- ORBIT — turn, thrust, shoot. Survive the wave.

Scoring is simple. One point per stage. Your best run on each cabinet is kept, then those are added together. The daily board is the top 10. At 00:00 UTC that board is paid and wiped.

You never click claim. If you finish in the top 10, the pot wallet sends ETH to the address you registered.

The pot is not a promise from thin air. It is a slice of real creator fees.

We launch $PONSCADE on Pons. Every trade pays creator fees in ETH. A bot claims those fees on a 15-minute cycle and splits them:

- 10% goes to a vault that is not the creator wallet. That vault is the daily pot. At midnight UTC it pays the top 10, then ranks reset.
- 10% buys $PONSCADE and sends it to the dead address. Supply shrinks.
- 80% buys $PONS and airdrops it to every wallet holding at least 1,500,000 $PONSCADE. Below that line, nothing.
